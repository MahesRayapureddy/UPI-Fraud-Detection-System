Learn About Top 5 UPI Scams

Phishing Scams

Fraudsters send deceptive links via SMS, email, or social media, tricking you into entering sensitive information like UPI PINs, OTPs, or bank details on fake websites.

How to Avoid:

  • Never click on suspicious links.
  • Always verify the URL of the website.
  • Look for "https://_blank" and the padlock icon in the address bar.

Collect Request Scams

Scammers send payment requests disguised as refunds, cashback, or debit reversals. They pressure you to enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money, which actually authorizes a debit from their account.

How to Avoid:

  • Never approve a collect request from an unknown person.
  • Remember, you don't need to enter a PIN to receive money.

Impersonation Scams

Fraudsters pose as bank officials, customer care representatives, or even genuine sellers. They manipulate victims into revealing sensitive financial information or downloading malicious applications.

How to Avoid:

  • Never share your PIN, OTP, or other sensitive data.
  • Banks or any legitimate company will never ask for this information.
  • If in doubt, hang up and call the official customer care number from the bank's website.

Screen Sharing Scams

Victims are convinced to download screen-sharing applications (like AnyDesk) under the pretext of resolving an issue or providing support. This grants scammers unauthorized access to their devices, allowing them to perform fraudulent transactions.

How to Avoid:

  • Never install any app at the request of an unknown person.
  • Do not grant screen sharing permissions to anyone you don't trust completely.

QR Code Scams

Fraudsters send fake QR codes or tamper with legitimate ones. When scanned, these codes lead you to unknowingly authorize payments to the scammer instead of receiving money or paying the intended recipient.

How to Avoid:

  • Be cautious when scanning QR codes from unknown sources.
  • Remember that scanning a QR code is for making payments, not receiving them.

UPI Safety - Do's and Don'ts

DO's

Verify UPI ID carefully

Always double-check the UPI ID before sending money

Set strong UPI PIN

Use a unique PIN and never share it with anyone

Enable app security

Use biometric authentication in your UPI app

Check transaction history

Regularly monitor your UPI transaction history

DON'Ts

Never share OTP/UPI PIN

No legitimate service will ask for your OTP or UPI PIN

Don't scan unknown QR codes

Avoid scanning QR codes from unknown sources

Avoid public Wi-Fi for transactions

Use mobile data or secure networks for UPI payments

Don't trust unsolicited calls

Hang up on calls claiming to be from bank support
